# OXS Trademark and Brand Policy

## OXS

**OXS** is the primary brand identifier used for software, systems and related projects created by Francisco Bexiga.

The principal expansion currently used is:

**OXS — Oeiras Xtreme Software**

OXS may also be used with other descriptive expansions or project areas where appropriate.

## Brand ownership

The names:

* OXS
* Oeiras Xtreme Software

together with associated logos, symbols, visual identity and project branding, are used and claimed by Francisco Bexiga as brand identifiers.

No trademark registration is represented or implied unless explicitly stated.

## Permitted references

You may use the OXS name only where reasonably necessary to:

* identify the original source of OXS software;
* comply with attribution requirements;
* refer factually to an OXS project;
* reproduce existing copyright, licence or attribution notices.

## Prohibited uses

Without prior written permission, you may not use OXS branding in a way that:

* suggests that your software or service is an official OXS product;
* suggests sponsorship, endorsement, certification or affiliation by OXS or Francisco Bexiga;
* uses OXS as the name or dominant branding of a modified or redistributed version;
* impersonates an official OXS project, website, repository, account or distribution;
* uses OXS branding for commercial promotion, resale or marketing;
* creates a confusingly similar name, logo or visual identity intended to imply an association with OXS.

## Forks and modified versions

Permitted forks or modified versions of OXS software must clearly state that they are unofficial modifications.

They must not be presented as official OXS releases.

Existing copyright, licence and attribution notices must be preserved.

## Logos and visual assets

OXS logos, icons, symbols and visual identity assets are not licensed for general reuse merely because they are visible in a public repository.

Specific logo or brand usage may require separate written permission.

## Software licence

The software licence and this brand policy are separate.

The OXS Non-Commercial Source License v1.0 governs use of the software.

It does not grant trademark or branding rights except for limited factual attribution.

## Registration status

OXS branding may be used with the `™` symbol where appropriate.

The `®` symbol must not be used unless and until the relevant mark is formally registered for the applicable territory and goods or services.

Requests for commercial licensing, brand permission or other authorisation should be made through an official OXS or project communication channel.
