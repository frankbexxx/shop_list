# OXS Brand Guide

## Brand

**OXS**

Primary institutional expansion:

**OXS — Oeiras Xtreme Software**

OXS is the primary brand identifier.

Other descriptive expansions such as Systems, Sound, Science or Search may be used where appropriate, but do not replace OXS as the core brand.

## Visual Direction

OXS 2026 uses an industrial-earth visual language combining:

* dark steel
* black and charcoal
* copper
* burnt orange
* terracotta
* toasted yellow
* dark red
* silver/metallic grey

The visual character is:

* industrial
* mechanical
* clockwork-inspired
* subtly steampunk
* grounded
* technical
* warm rather than neon
* dark without becoming visually oppressive

Avoid:

* bright neon
* glossy tech gradients
* generic blue technology branding
* excessive gold
* excessive steampunk ornament
* overly decorative mechanical elements
